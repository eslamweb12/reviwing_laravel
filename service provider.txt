what is the service provider ? 

In Laravel, a service provider is a crucial part of the framework's service container and dependency injection system.
Service providers are the central place to configure and bootstrap your application. They are responsible for binding 
classes or interfaces into the service container, registering event listeners, middleware, and more.

and you can make your provider by run this commnd =>php artisan make:provider PaymentServiceProvider

and you should register it in the providers in the bootstrpa like this
<?php

return [
    App\Providers\AppServiceProvider::class,
    App\Providers\PaymentServiceProvider::class,
];
